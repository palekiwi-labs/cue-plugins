# Research Task Sessions (`kind: research`)

A research task answers open questions. It produces knowledge, not code
and not specifications.

## Purpose and Outputs

- **Purpose**: Explore ideas, evaluate tools and dependencies, test
  hypotheses, and answer technical unknowns.
- **Outputs**: Research documents (`doc/`), diagnostic traces (`trace/`),
  exploration notes (`note/`), and milestone history (`log.md`).

## Completion

A research task is complete when the questions it was opened to answer
have been answered, or established as unanswerable by research alone.

## Boundaries

- Do not write feature implementation code.
- Do not draft specifications for the target repository. That is a design
  task.
